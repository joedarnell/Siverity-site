Mochi Lane support

Something not working, or have an idea? Email info@siverity.com (info@siverity.com). We usually reply within two business days.

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include your phone model, its operating system version, the game version from the settings screen, and the level you were playing.

Common questions

How do lives work?

You have up to 5 lives. Starting a level uses one; winning gives it back. Lost lives refill at one every 30 minutes.

I'm stuck on a level.

Every level can be beaten. After a few tries, Kitsu will quietly lend a paw. Try making power pieces early: 4 in a row makes a Katana, an L or T shape makes a Fox Spirit, and 5 in a row makes a Paper Lantern. Combining two power pieces clears big parts of the board.

How do I rebuild the street?

Each star you earn can be spent on an upgrade for the current chapter's street. Every chapter has 7 upgrades; finish them all to open the next chapter.

I lost my progress.

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes it. Email us with your device details and the level you reached and we'll do what we can.

I bought something and didn't receive it.

Close and reopen the game, then use Restore purchases in settings. If it's still missing, email us. Payments are handled by Apple or Google, so refund requests go to them: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or Google Play through your order history.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads in the shop. It's a one-time purchase that removes ads between levels for good. Reward videos stay available if you choose to watch them for a bonus.
