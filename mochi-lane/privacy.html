Mochi Lane privacy policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the Mochi Lane: Match 3 Puzzle mobile game ("the game"), made by Siverity Software LLC ("we", "us"), collects and how it is used. If you have questions, email info@siverity.com.

The short version

  • You don't create an account, and we don't ask for your name, email, location or contacts.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • The free version shows ads. Our advertising partner may collect a device advertising identifier and basic device information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your level, stars, street upgrades, lives, coins, purchases and settings (such as sound on or off) on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This information is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device type, operating system, and information about the ads you see and tap, to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance, and prevent fraud. On iPhone, we ask your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking, and you can decline. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Google's use of this information is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ads appear from Chapter 4 onward. Buying Remove Ads turns off ads between levels. Reward videos are always optional and only play if you choose to watch one.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. They process your payment under their own privacy policies and tell the game only whether a purchase succeeded, so we can unlock what you bought.

Crash and performance information

If the game crashes, Apple or Google may share anonymous crash reports with us, depending on your device settings. These help us fix bugs and do not identify you.

Children

The game is rated for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your choices and rights

You can limit ad tracking in your device settings, decline the tracking prompt on iPhone, buy Remove ads, or delete the app to remove all locally stored data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal information; because we don't hold personal information about you ourselves, requests about ad data are best directed to Google, but we are happy to help at info@siverity.com.

We don't sell your information

We do not sell personal information. Ad data is handled by our advertising partner as described above.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and its effective date.
