Mochi Lane: Match 3 Puzzle

Match cute snacks, rebuild a cozy street and uncover Grandma's secret story.

Hana inherits her late grandma Aiko's little snack shop on Mochi Lane, once the coziest street in town and now dusty and shuttered. With Kitsu, a mischievous two-tailed fox spirit, she sets out to bring the street back to life, and to find every page of Grandma Aiko's lost recipe notebook.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Rated for ages 13 and up.

How it plays

Swap onigiri, dango, ramen, taiyaki and more to clear each board's goals in a limited number of moves or against the clock. Match 4 for a Katana, an L or T for a Fox Spirit, and 5 for a Paper Lantern, then combine them for big combos.

Rebuild the street

Every star you earn fixes something up: new shop curtains, paper lanterns, a festival stage, a sushi counter. 230 levels across 16 chapters, from spring festivals to New Year on Mochi Lane.

Fair and friendly

Free to play with 5 lives. Kitsu lends a paw if you're stuck. Every purchase shows exactly what it contains, with no loot boxes, and a one-time Remove Ads purchase.
