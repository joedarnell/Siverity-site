Mochi Ghost: Spooky Runner

A cute-spooky runner starring Mochi, a little ghost knight. Float through the Haunted Bamboo Forest and the Graveyard, slash monsters, block their attacks with your shield, and face a giant boss at the end of every world.

Free to play, with optional purchases. No sign-in. Made for phones and tablets, coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

How it plays

The world scrolls by on its own. Hold the left side of the screen to float, tap the right side to slash, and hold it to raise your shield, which bounces shots straight back. Fill the spirit meter and tap the glowing button for a Spirit Burst. Earn up to three stars in every level by reaching the end, finding all three hidden scrolls, and getting through without a scratch.

Two worlds, 50 levels

Start in the Haunted Bamboo Forest, with low branches and narrow passages, then head into the Graveyard, where tombstones rise and hands reach up from the ground. Each world has 25 levels, a mini-boss halfway and a giant boss at the end, from the Possessed Scarecrow to the Giant Skull. More worlds are on the way.

Outfits and hats

Mochi starts as a Knight with a Shield Rush, and the Samurai, with a storm of blades, unlocks free when you clear the first world. Collect soul orbs to upgrade your hearts, sword, shield and Spirit Burst, and dress Mochi up with hats from the Wardrobe. Cute and spooky, never gory: made for players 13 and up.

Free to play

There are no lives or energy timers: if you fall, just try again. A short video gives you a free revive in each level. After 5 levels, a short ad appears between some levels, never in the middle of one, and a one-time purchase removes them. Everything in the shop shows exactly what you get, with no random rewards.
