Mochi Ghost support

Answers to common questions about Mochi Ghost, and how to reach us.

Contact support

Something not working, or have an idea? Email info@siverity.com (info@siverity.com). We usually reply within two business days.

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include your phone model, its operating system version, the game version from the settings screen, and the level you were playing.

Common questions

How do I play?

Mochi floats forward on his own. Hold the left side of the screen to float up, and let go to drift down. Tap the right side to slash, and hold it to raise your shield, which blocks attacks and bounces shots back. When the spirit meter is full, tap the glowing button for a Spirit Burst.

How do I get three stars?

You earn one star for reaching the end, one for finding all three hidden scrolls, and one for finishing without getting hit. You can replay any level to improve your stars.

What happens if I get hit too often?

You lose a heart each time you're hit; heart candles along the way refill them. Run out and you can revive once per level by watching a short video, and a second time for 5 gems. Or just try again: there are no lives or energy timers, so you can replay as often as you like.

How do I get new outfits and hats?

The Knight is Mochi's starting outfit. The Samurai unlocks free when you beat the World 1 boss, or you can get it early for gems. Hats cost 10 to 25 gems in the Wardrobe and can be worn with any outfit.

What are orbs and gems?

Soul orbs are collected in every level and pay for upgrades: more hearts, a longer sword reach, a stronger shield and a faster Spirit Burst. Gems are rarer: you find them in levels and can also buy them in the Gem store. They pay for hats, outfits and a second revive.

I lost my progress.

Progress, orbs, gems and outfits are saved only on your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes them. A Remove Ads purchase can always be brought back with Restore purchases. There are no accounts, so we can't restore progress from our side, but email us if something went wrong and we'll help where we can.

Are there ads or purchases?

Mochi Ghost is free to play. After you've cleared 5 levels, a short ad appears between some levels, never in the middle of one. You can buy gem packs and Remove Ads in the Gem store, but you never need to buy anything to finish a level.

How do I remove ads or restore a purchase?

Open the Gem store and buy Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase that stops the ads between levels for good. Reward videos stay available whenever you want a free revive. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the Gem store and tap Restore purchases.

I was charged but didn't get my gems.

Close and reopen the game, then check the Gem store. If they're still missing, email us with the date of the purchase and your order number from Google Play or Apple. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.
