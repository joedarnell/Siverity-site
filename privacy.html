Privacy policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile games made by Siverity Software LLC ("we", "us") collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to every game listed below ("the games"). Questions: info@siverity.com.

Games covered

  • Mochi Lane: Match 3 Puzzle
  • Mochi Lane Tiles
  • Sushidoku
  • Puni Pals: Chibi Block Escape
  • Cat Commute

All of these games work the same way where privacy is concerned. If a future game collects anything not described here, we will update this policy before that game is released.

The short version

  • You don't create an account, and we don't ask for your name, address, phone number, location or contacts.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device.
  • The games show ads through Google AdMob, and use Google Firebase to count how the games are played and to report crashes.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.

How we use it

  • Run the games: save your progress, deliver what you buy, restore purchases, refill lives on time.
  • Fix and improve them: find crashes, see which levels are too hard or too easy, decide what to build next.
  • Show ads: ads between levels, and optional reward videos you choose to watch. Ads are personalized only if you allow it (see Your choices).
  • Send notifications you allowed, such as when new levels arrive or your lives are full.
  • Answer support requests, prevent fraud and cheating, and meet legal obligations.

Legal bases (EU/UK players): we process data to provide the game you asked for (contract), for our legitimate interest in keeping it working and improving it, and with your consent for personalized ads and tracking. You can withdraw consent at any time.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect players or our rights, or as part of a sale or merger of the business (the new owner would keep this policy's promises).

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some U.S. state laws treat sharing an advertising ID with ad networks for personalized ads as a "sale" or "sharing." You can opt out of that (see Your choices).

Children

The games are made for players aged 13 and up and are not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in some countries). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Ad tracking: on iPhone, choose "Ask App Not to Track" when prompted, or change it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, delete or reset your Advertising ID in your device settings. You'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized. In the EU, the UK and some U.S. states, the games also show Google's consent form, and you can reopen it from the game's settings ("Ad privacy choices").
  • Notifications: turn them off in the game's settings or your device settings.
  • Remove ads: the "Remove Ads" purchase turns off ads between levels. Reward videos stay optional.
  • Access, correct or delete your data: email info@siverity.com. If you uninstall a game, data saved only on your device is deleted.

Your rights by location. Depending on where you live (for example California and other U.S. states, the EU, the UK), you may have the right to know what we hold about you, get a copy, correct it, delete it, opt out of targeted advertising or "sale/sharing," and not be treated differently for using these rights. EU/UK players can also complain to their local data protection authority. We answer requests within the time the law requires.

How long we keep it

Game progress stays on your device until you delete the game. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months, and crash reports for about 90 days. Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting laws require, and support emails for up to 24 months. Ad data held by Google is kept under Google's policies.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, such as encrypted connections and limited access. No system is perfectly secure.

International transfers

We and our partners may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses are used.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update this page and its effective date, and tell you in the games before the changes take effect.
