Puni Pals: Chibi Block Escape

A cozy sliding-block puzzle starring a crew of squishy chibi animals: a bunny, fox, chick, frog, kitty and bear. Each pal needs to get home through the gate that matches its colour and animal, but the board is packed and everyone's in each other's way.

No ads. No purchases. No sign-in. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

How it plays

Drag the pals around the board, work out who has to leave first, and clear the board before time runs out. Big pals need wide gates, so line them up carefully. Earn up to three stars on every level.

New twists keep coming

Eight tutorial levels teach the basics. Then come stones that never move, pals that only slide one way, frozen pals that thaw as friends leave, paint puddles, keys and locks, and blinking gates, all the way from Tutorial Garden to Starlight Summit.

Easy on everyone

Every colour is also a different animal, so the game is easy to play if you're colourblind. Gentle, non-violent fun, with nothing to buy and no ads.
