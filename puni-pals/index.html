Puni Pals: Chibi Block Escape

A cozy sliding-block puzzle starring a crew of squishy chibi animals: a bunny, fox, chick, frog, kitty and bear. Each pal needs to get home through the gate that matches its colour and animal, but the board is packed and everyone's in each other's way.

Free to play, with optional purchases. No sign-in. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

How it plays

Drag the pals around the board, work out who has to leave first, and clear the board before time runs out. Big pals need wide gates, so line them up carefully. Earn up to three stars on every level.

New twists keep coming

Eight tutorial levels teach the basics. Then come stones that never move, pals that only slide one way, frozen pals that thaw as friends leave, paint puddles, keys and locks, and blinking gates, all the way from Tutorial Garden to Starlight Summit. Every 10th level is Hard, every 50th is Super hard, and there's a new daily puzzle every day.

Easy on everyone

Every colour is also a different animal, so the game is easy to play if you're colourblind. Gentle, non-violent fun for players 13 and up.

Free to play

Earn coins as you play and spend them on hints, extra time and Pops. After level 12, a short ad appears between some levels, never in the middle of a puzzle, and a one-time purchase removes them. Everything in the shop shows exactly what you get, with no random rewards.
