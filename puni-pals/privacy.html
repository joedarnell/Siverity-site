Puni Pals privacy policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the Puni Pals: Chibi Block Escape mobile game ("the game"), made by Siverity Software LLC ("we", "us"). If you have questions, email info@siverity.com.

The short version

The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no sign-in.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, stars and settings on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

No ads, no tracking, no purchases

The game contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and nothing to buy. It does not use your device's advertising identifier, and it does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, Apple or Google may share anonymous crash reports with us, depending on your device settings. These help us fix bugs and do not identify you.

Children

The game has gentle, non-violent content and is made for players 13 and up. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the game handles information, for example by adding a feature that needs data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
