Puni Pals support

Answers to common questions about Puni Pals, and how to reach us.

Contact support

Something not working, or have an idea? Email info@siverity.com (info@siverity.com). We usually reply within two business days.

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include your phone model, its operating system version, the game version from the settings screen, and the level you were playing.

Common questions

How do I play?

Drag a pal to slide it around the board. Each pal leaves through the gate that matches its colour and animal. Work out who needs to leave first, and clear the board before the timer runs out. Big pals need wide gates.

What do the special pieces do?

Stones never move. Arrow pals only slide one way. Frozen pals thaw as their friends leave. Paint puddles change a pal's colour, keys open locks, and blinking gates open and shut, so time your moves.

How do I get three stars?

Clear the level with time to spare. The faster you finish, the more stars you earn. You can replay any level to improve your stars.

I'm colourblind. Can I play?

Yes. Every colour is also a different animal (bunny, fox, chick, frog, kitty and bear), so you can always match a pal to its gate by the animal.

I lost my progress.

Progress, coins and boosters are saved only on your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes them. A Remove Ads purchase can always be brought back with Restore purchases. There are no accounts, so we can't restore it from our side, but email us if something went wrong and we'll help where we can.

Are there ads or purchases?

Puni Pals is free to play. After you've cleared 12 levels, a short ad appears between some levels, never in the middle of a puzzle. You can buy coin packs and Remove Ads in the shop, but you never need to buy anything to finish a level, and every paid extra also has a free way to get it.

How do coins and boosters work?

You earn 10 coins for the first win of a level (20 on a Hard level, 35 on a Super hard level), 5 more the first time you get 3 stars, and 2 for replaying a level you've already beaten. Spend them on a hint (100 coins, shows a pal that can leave), a +20 second time boost (150), or a Pop (250, sends any pal you tap straight home). You can also get 2 free boosters a day by watching a short video.

How do lives work?

You have up to 5 lives, and one comes back every 30 minutes. Running out of time uses 1 life, and so does leaving a level partway through (the game asks first). Winning never costs a life, and the daily puzzle is always free. When you're out, you can wait, watch a short video for 1 life (twice a day), or refill all 5 for 600 coins.

What happens when time runs out?

If at least half the pals are already home, you can keep going with 20 more seconds: use a time boost, watch a video (once per level), or spend coins (300, then 600, then 1,000). With fewer than half home, just try again.

What's the daily gift?

Open the game each day to collect a gift from a 7-day calendar of coins, boosters and unlimited lives. On coin days you can watch a short video to double the coins. Miss a day and your calendar simply waits for you.

How do I remove ads or restore a purchase?

Open the Shop and buy Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase that stops the ads between levels for good. Reward videos stay available whenever you want a free extra. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the Shop and tap Restore purchases.

I was charged but didn't get my coins.

Close and reopen the game, then check the Shop. If they're still missing, email us with the date of the purchase and your order number from Google Play or Apple. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.
