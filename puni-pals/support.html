Puni Pals support

Something not working, or have an idea? Email info@siverity.com (info@siverity.com). We usually reply within two business days.

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include your phone model, its operating system version, the game version from the settings screen, and the level you were playing.

Common questions

How do I play?

Drag a pal to slide it around the board. Each pal leaves through the gate that matches its colour and animal. Work out who needs to leave first, and clear the board before the timer runs out. Big pals need wide gates.

What do the special pieces do?

Stones never move. Arrow pals only slide one way. Frozen pals thaw as their friends leave. Paint puddles change a pal's colour, keys open locks, and blinking gates open and shut, so time your moves.

How do I get three stars?

Clear the level with time to spare. The faster you finish, the more stars you earn. You can replay any level to improve your stars.

I'm colourblind. Can I play?

Yes. Every colour is also a different animal (bunny, fox, chick, frog, kitty and bear), so you can always match a pal to its gate by the animal.

I lost my progress.

Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes it. There are no accounts, so we can't restore it from our side, but email us if something went wrong and we'll help where we can.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. Puni Pals has no ads and nothing to buy.
