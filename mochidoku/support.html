Mochidoku support

Answers to common questions about Mochidoku, and how to reach us.

Contact support

Something not working, or have an idea? Email info@siverity.com (info@siverity.com). We usually reply within two business days.

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include your phone model, its operating system version, the game version from the settings screen, and the level you were playing.

Common questions

How do I play?

Tap a square once for an X, twice for a mochi and three times to clear it. You can drag to mark X's quickly. Every row, every column and every coloured tray needs exactly one mochi, and no two mochi can touch, not even diagonally.

What happens if I place a mochi in the wrong spot?

It slides off and uses one of your 3 mistakes for that level. Use all 3 and the level ends, so you can try again.

I'm stuck. How do the boosters work?

Kitsu's boosters help: Sweep crosses out squares that can't hold a mochi, Hint places a mochi for you, and Kitsu's Paw places two. You can also replay Hana's lessons any time from Settings.

I'm colourblind. Can I play?

Yes. Turn on Tray patterns in Settings and every tray gets its own pattern as well as its colour.

How do lives work?

You have up to 5 lives, and one comes back every 30 minutes. Failing a level uses 1 life, and so does leaving a level partway through. Winning never costs a life. When you're out, you can wait, watch a short video for a life, or refill with coins.

I lost my progress.

Progress, coins and boosters are saved only on your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes them. One-time purchases such as Remove Ads and board styles can always be brought back with Restore purchases. There are no accounts, so we can't restore progress from our side, but email us if something went wrong and we'll help where we can.

Are there ads or purchases?

Mochidoku is free to play. A short ad appears between some levels, never in the middle of a puzzle. You can buy coin packs, board styles and Remove Ads in the shop, but you never need to buy anything to finish a level, and every paid extra also has a free way to get it.

How do I remove ads or restore a purchase?

Open Hana's shop and buy Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase that stops the ads between levels for good. Reward videos stay available whenever you want a free extra. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open Settings and tap Restore purchases.

I was charged but didn't get my item.

Close and reopen the game, then check the shop. If it's still missing, email us with the date of the purchase and your order number from Google Play or Apple. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.
