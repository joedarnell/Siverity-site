Mochidoku: Mochi Logic Puzzle

A cozy logic puzzle from the world of Mochi Lane. Help Hana set out the mochi in her shop: one in every row, every column and every coloured tray, and no two mochi may touch, not even at the corners.

Free to play, with optional purchases. No sign-in. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

How it plays

Tap a square once to mark an X and twice to place a mochi. Every row, column and coloured tray gets exactly one mochi, and mochi are sticky, so no two can touch. Three mistakes are allowed per level. Earn up to three stars on every board.

Learn as you go

Hana teaches a new trick whenever you need one, from “last free square” to trays that share two rows. Boards grow from small starters to busy 8×8 shops, with Hard and Super hard levels and a daily treat puzzle.

Easy on everyone

Turn on tray patterns in Settings so the trays are easy to tell apart if you're colourblind. Auto-mark X's around each mochi if you'd like a helping hand. Gentle, non-violent fun for players 13 and up.

Free to play

Earn coins as you play and spend them on Kitsu's boosters: Sweep, Hint and Kitsu's Paw. A short ad appears between some levels, never in the middle of a puzzle, and a one-time purchase removes them. Board styles are optional extras, and everything in the shop shows exactly what you get.
