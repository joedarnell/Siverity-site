Cat Commute support

Something not working, or have an idea? Email info@siverity.com (info@siverity.com). We usually reply within two business days.

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include your phone model, its operating system version (for example iOS 19 or Android 16), the game version from the settings screen, and the level you were playing.

Common questions

How do I play?

Each carrier slides out the way its wire door faces, but only if nothing is in its path. Send carriers to the pickup spots at the top. Cats in line climb into a carrier of their own colour, and a full carrier heads home. Get every cat home to clear the level.

I'm stuck on a level. Is it possible?

Yes. Every level can be solved. If you run out of pickup spots, use the Extra spot booster or the Magic paw, or retry and think about which carriers the next cats in line need.

I lost my progress.

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes it. Email us with your device details and the level you reached and we'll do what we can.

I bought something and didn't receive it.

First, close and reopen the game, and use Restore purchases in settings. If it's still missing, email us. Payments are handled by Apple or Google, so refund requests go to them: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or Google Play through your order history.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove ads in the game's shop. It removes ads between levels for good. You can still choose to watch an ad for a free booster.
