Cat Commute

Every cat rides home in a carrier of its own colour. Tap a carrier to slide it out of the crowded room and onto a pickup spot, but plan ahead: fill every spot with the wrong colours and it's a cat-astrophe.

How to play

Each carrier slides the way its door faces. Clear the way, send carriers to the pickup spots, and the matching cats climb in. A full carrier heads home.

Boosters

Stuck? Add an extra pickup spot, or use the magic paw to lift any carrier straight out, even a blocked one.
