Logic Gear Sort

A logic puzzle built from meshing gears and bold colors. Turn a gear and every gear it touches turns the other way. Swap pegs where the teeth meet, and sort every gear to a single color.

Free to play, with optional purchases. No sign-in. Made for phones and tablets, coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

How it plays

Every gear holds colored pegs. Tap a gear to turn it one step, and the gears it touches turn the other way, so nothing moves on its own. Where two gears meet, tap the swap button to trade the two pegs facing each other. Make every gear one color, and finish at or under par for three stars.

8 chapters, 200 levels

Start with two gears and work up to seven. Each chapter adds something new: locked gears that only turn when a neighbor does, big 12-peg gears that turn half as far, and one-way gears that only ever turn clockwise. Every level is checked to be solvable.

Help when you're stuck

A Hint shows the next good move, Clutch turns one gear on its own, and Unlock frees a locked or one-way gear. You get five free undos in every level. Every color also has its own shape, so the puzzles work for colorblind players too.

Free to play

There are no lives or energy timers: play as long as you like. After you've cleared 15 levels, a short ad appears after some wins, never in the middle of a level, and a one-time purchase removes them. Everything in the shop shows exactly what you get, with no random rewards.
