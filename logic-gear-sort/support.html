Common questions

How do I play?

Tap a gear to turn it one step. Every gear touching it turns one step the other way. To turn a gear the other direction, drag your finger around it, or flip the "Tap turns" button. Where two gears meet, tap the round swap button to trade the two pegs facing each other there. Make every gear one color to win.

How do I get three stars?

Each level has a par, the number of moves we know it can be solved in. Finish at or under par for three stars, a little over par for two, and any finish for one. Using a booster caps the level at two stars. You can replay any level to improve.

What do the special gears do?

Locked gears (padlock) can't be tapped, but they still turn when a neighbor turns. Big gears hold 12 pegs, turn half as far per step, and need 12 of one color. One-way gears (black center with an arrow) only turn clockwise, so a move that would turn one backwards is blocked.

I'm stuck. What can I do?

Use a Hint to see the next good move; it works even after wrong moves. Clutch turns one gear without its neighbors, and Unlock frees a locked or one-way gear for the rest of the level. You also get five free undos in every level, and Restart is always free. Every level can be solved without boosters.

How do I get coins and boosters?

You earn coins for every level you clear, with a bonus for three stars or beating par. You can also watch an optional video for free coins or a free booster, or buy coins in the shop. Coins buy Hints, Clutches, Unlocks and extra undos.

I lost my progress.

Progress, coins, boosters and themes are saved only on your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes them. Remove Ads and gear themes can always be brought back with Restore purchases. There are no accounts, so we can't restore progress from our side, but email us if something went wrong and we'll help where we can.

Are there ads or purchases?

Logic Gear Sort is free to play, with no lives or energy timers. After you've cleared 15 levels, a short ad appears after some wins, never in the middle of a level. You can buy coins, gear themes and Remove Ads in the shop, but you never need to buy anything to finish a level.

How do I remove ads or restore a purchase?

Open the shop and buy Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase that stops the ads between levels for good. Optional videos stay available whenever you want free coins or a booster. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the shop and tap Restore purchases.

I was charged but didn't get my coins.

Close and reopen the game, then check the shop. If they're still missing, email us with the date of the purchase and your order number from Google Play or Apple. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.
