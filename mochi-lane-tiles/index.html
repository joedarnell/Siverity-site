Mochi Lane Tiles

A cozy triple tile match from the world of Mochi Lane. Tap snack tiles from the pile into your tray, match three alike to clear them, and work your way through 200 levels across eight chapters, from Hana's Snack Shop to the Fox Shrine on the Hill.

Free to play, with optional purchases. No sign-in. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

How it plays

Tiles are stacked in layers, and only the uncovered ones can be tapped. Each tile you tap goes into a 7-slot tray, and three alike clear away. Clear the whole pile to win, but fill all 7 slots without a match and the level is lost. Earn up to three stars by winning without boosters.

Eight chapters to explore

200 levels across eight chapters, each hosted by a Mochi Lane friend: Hana's Snack Shop, Ren's Ramen Stand, the Sakura Festival, a harbour sushi counter and more. Every 5th level is Hard and each chapter ends with a Super hard level. Every level is checked to be winnable without boosters.

Helpful boosters

Undo sends your last tile back, Shuffle remixes the pile, and Kitsu's Paw pulls out a full matching set, even from buried tiles. You start with a few of each and earn more as you play.

Free to play

Earn coins as you play and spend them on boosters. After level 12, a short ad appears between some levels, never in the middle of a puzzle, and a one-time purchase removes them. Everything in the shop shows exactly what you get, with no random rewards.
