Mochi Lane Tiles support

Answers to common questions about Mochi Lane Tiles, and how to reach us.

Contact support

Something not working, or have an idea? Email info@siverity.com (info@siverity.com). We usually reply within two business days.

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include your phone model, its operating system version, the game version from the settings screen, and the level you were playing.

Common questions

How do I play?

Tap any tile that isn't covered by another tile. It moves into your tray, and three matching tiles clear away. Clear every tile from the pile to win. If all 7 tray slots fill up without a match, the level is lost.

How do I get three stars?

Win the level without using any boosters. Using one booster earns two stars, and more earns one star. You can replay any level to improve your stars.

What do the boosters do?

Undo returns your last tile to the pile. Shuffle remixes the tiles. Kitsu's Paw pulls a full matching set of three straight into your tray, even tiles that are buried.

How do lives work?

You have up to 5 lives, and one comes back every 30 minutes. Losing a level uses 1 life, and so does leaving a level partway through (the game asks first). Winning never costs a life. When you're out, you can wait, watch a short video for 1 life (twice a day), or refill all 5 for 600 coins.

What's the daily gift?

Open the game each day to collect a gift from a 7-day calendar of coins, boosters and unlimited lives. Miss a day and your calendar simply waits for you.

I lost my progress.

Progress, coins and boosters are saved only on your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes them. A Remove Ads purchase can always be brought back with Restore purchases. There are no accounts, so we can't restore progress from our side, but email us if something went wrong and we'll help where we can.

Are there ads or purchases?

Mochi Lane Tiles is free to play. After you've cleared 12 levels, a short ad appears between some levels, never in the middle of a puzzle. You can buy coin packs and Remove Ads in the shop, but you never need to buy anything to finish a level, and every paid extra also has a free way to get it.

How do I remove ads or restore a purchase?

Open the shop and buy Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase that stops the ads between levels for good. Reward videos stay available whenever you want a free extra. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open Settings and tap Restore purchases.

I was charged but didn't get my coins.

Close and reopen the game, then check the shop. If they're still missing, email us with the date of the purchase and your order number from Google Play or Apple. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.
